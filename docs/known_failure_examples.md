# Known failure modes to inspect

1. A dramatic word such as “secret” or “betrayal” can receive a high lexical score even when it is not the actual unresolved final beat.
2. A candidate may begin on a strong line but lack enough character context for a first-time viewer.
3. Screenplay camera/action descriptions are not proof that the filmed footage has the same visual impact.
4. A continuous-window fallback cannot create a cross-episode montage and may include low-energy seconds between two strong beats.

These are intentionally surfaced rather than hidden. Use the evidence excerpt and original TXT to reject poor candidates.
